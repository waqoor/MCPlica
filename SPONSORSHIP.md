# Sponsoring MCPlica

Sponsorship supports maintenance, security work, documentation, and public infrastructure. It never purchases governance authority, roadmap priority, private product access, release approval, licensing exceptions, or preferential vulnerability handling.

No official funding channel is configured in this repository at present. `.github/FUNDING.yml` therefore intentionally contains no account. When the founder enables a verified channel, it must be recorded there before the project advertises it as official.

Sponsors may be acknowledged with their consent. Acknowledgement does not imply endorsement, certification, or a service-level agreement. The authoritative sustainability and independence rules are in `docs/open_source_and_sponsorship_model.md`.
